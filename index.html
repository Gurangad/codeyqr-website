So, what the hell is CodeYQR?

CodeYQR is a student-led initiative creating more opportunities for young people to explore technology, develop practical skills, and showcase what they can build.

💻

Build

Turn weird ideas into real projects — apps, games, art, whatever you're into.

🧠

Learn

Try new things, learn new skills, and figure stuff out as you go. Nobody expects you to already know it.

🤝

Find Your People

Meet other students who are just as interested in building cool things as you are.

No experience? No problem.

You don't need to have been coding since you were six. Whether you've never opened a code editor, love designing things, have a crazy idea, or already build projects for fun — there's a place for you here.

our track record

We've done this before.

Before CodeYQR, our team organized Campfire Regina — the first Hack Club hackathon held in Saskatchewan. It was a small event, but it gave our student organizing team firsthand experience planning a technology event from the ground up: promotion, logistics, volunteers, food, prizes, and event operations.

12,000+
social media impressions
200+
Instagram followers

Now we're taking what we learned and building something bigger.

Our next event is designed around those lessons — a stronger recruitment strategy, a larger volunteer and mentor network, and a more structured participant experience.

Where things stand: CodeYQR works with Glocal to support a community-driven, volunteer-based model. We're currently awaiting approval to join Hack Club Bank, which — if approved — would provide financial infrastructure and nonprofit fiscal sponsorship for our events.

FAQ

What is a hackathon?+

A hackathon is an event where people team up to build a project — an app, game, website, whatever — over a set amount of time. You don't need to finish something perfect. You just need to build something.

Do I need coding experience?+

No. Beginners are genuinely welcome — there's mentorship on hand and plenty of first-timers at every event.

Who can participate?+

Students in Grades 8–12 in Regina and the surrounding area.

How much does it cost?+

Our goal is for the event to be accessible to students. Exact pricing details will be confirmed when registration opens.

Can I come without a team?+

Yes — plenty of people show up solo and team up on the day.

When does registration open?+

Coming soon — join CodeYQR or follow us on Instagram to be the first to know.

Where will the event happen?+

Coming soon — we're finalizing venue options in Regina.

Can beginners actually participate?+

Absolutely. This is one of the most important things we want people to know.
